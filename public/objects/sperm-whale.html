    About the sperm whale

    The sperm whale is the largest of the toothed whales and one of the most widely distributed marine mammals. NOAA Fisheries lists males at about 52 ft long and 45 tons, roughly three times the weight of females, which reach about 40 ft and 15 tons. Sperm whales hunt squid, sharks, skates and fish on deep dives that routinely reach 2,000 ft.

    • Sperm whales are the largest of the toothed whales and have one of the widest global distributions of any marine mammal. [1]
    • Their deep feeding dives routinely reach 2,000 ft and can last 45 minutes. [1]
    • For their first 10 years males are only slightly larger than females, but they keep growing substantially into their 30s. [1]
    • Females reach sexual maturity at about 9 years old, when roughly 29 ft long. Sperm whales can live up to 60 years. [1]
