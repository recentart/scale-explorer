    About the mid-size car

    Passenger cars come in many sizes, so this page uses one of the best-selling mid-size sedans in the United States, the 2026 Toyota Camry, as a well-documented example. Toyota lists it at 193.5 inches (about 4.9 m) long and 56.9 inches tall. Compact cars are shorter, while pickup trucks and large SUVs are longer.

    Notes on these figures

    • This is one example car, not an average. Toyota.com lists these dimensions for the XLE and Nightshade all-wheel-drive grades; small hatchbacks are about a metre shorter and full-size pickup trucks longer.
