    About the giant cruise ship

    Legend of the Seas, the third Icon-class ship built by Meyer Turku in Finland for Royal Caribbean, is one of the largest cruise ships ever built. The shipyard lists it as 365 m long, with a gross tonnage of 250,800 and room for 5,610 passengers. Gross tonnage measures a ship's enclosed volume, not its weight.

    • Meyer Turku lists Legend of the Seas at 250,800 gross tonnage and 5,610 passengers. [1]
