    About the Spinosaurus

    Spinosaurus was a large meat-eating dinosaur from North Africa that lived about 99 to 94 million years ago in the Late Cretaceous. It had a narrow, fish-catching snout, a tall sail on its back and short hind legs, and may have spent much of its time in and around water. It is thought to be the longest known carnivorous dinosaur, with length estimates of about 14 to 18 metres, but no complete skeleton has been found.

    • The first Spinosaurus fossils, found in Egypt in 1912 and named by Ernst Stromer in 1915, were destroyed in April 1944 when a bombing raid hit the Munich museum that held them. [1] [2]
    • Its short hind legs meant that Tyrannosaurus stood taller at the hips, even though Spinosaurus was longer. [1]
    • It had a wide, paddle-like tail, and scientists still debate how much it swam and whether it walked on two legs or four. [1]
    • Later fragmentary remains have been found in Algeria, Morocco, Tunisia and Niger. [2]
