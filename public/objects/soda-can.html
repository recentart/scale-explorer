    About the soda can

    The standard North American drink can holds 12 US fluid ounces (355 mL), with a separate, narrower lid. Can maker Ball Corporation's industry-standard drawing gives the aluminum body a 2.600 in (66.04 mm) outside diameter and a height of 4.812 in (122.22 mm). The top narrows to a smaller neck so that a smaller lid can be used.

    • Ball's standard 12 oz can is designated 202/211 x 413: a 211 body diameter, a 413 height and a necked-in 202 lid. [1]
    • The neck at the top of the can has a plug diameter of 2.063 in (52.40 mm), narrower than the 2.600 in body. [1]

    Notes on these figures

    • Ball's height is for the open, flanged can body before the lid is seamed on; no primary source was found for the height of a sealed can, which differs slightly.
    • No can-maker source for the weight of an empty or full can was found, so mass is left out.
    • The Ball drawing is hosted on a distributor's site (CanSource), not on ball.com.
