    About Merdeka 118

    Merdeka 118 is a 118-storey megatall skyscraper in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, completed in 2023; the Skyscraper Center now lists it as Menara Merdeka Maybank. Its architectural height, including the spire, is 678.9 m (2,227 ft), and its tip reaches 680.5 m. It is the second-tallest building in the world after the Burj Khalifa and the tallest in Asia.

    • It has 118 floors above ground and 5 below ground; construction started in 2014 and it was completed in 2023. [1]
    • The Skyscraper Center ranks it the second-tallest building in the world and the tallest in Asia, Malaysia and Kuala Lumpur (September 2026). [1]
    • Its core walls and mega-columns are reinforced concrete without embedded structural steel except at the outrigger trusses, which cut imported steel costs. [1]
    • The developer is Permodalan Nasional Berhad, and the listed tenants include Malayan Banking Berhad (Maybank). [1]
