    About the soccer pitch

    Soccer pitches do not have one fixed size. The Laws of the Game allow touchlines from 90 to 120 m in general, but international matches must be played on a pitch 100 to 110 m long and 64 to 75 m wide. The goals are 7.32 m (8 yd) wide between the posts and 2.44 m (8 ft) high to the underside of the crossbar. The penalty mark is 11 m from the goal.

    • The penalty area reaches 16.5 m (18 yd) out from the inside of each goalpost and 16.5 m into the field; the goal area is 5.5 m (6 yd) on the same pattern. [1]
    • Goalposts and the crossbar may be no more than 12 cm (5 in) wide, and all pitch lines share one width of no more than 12 cm. [1]
    • Competitions may choose their own touchline and goal line lengths as long as they stay within the Law 1 limits. [1]
