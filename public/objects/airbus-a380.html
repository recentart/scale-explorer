    About the Airbus A380

    The Airbus A380 is a double-deck, four-engine airliner and the largest passenger aircraft built. Airbus's airport planning document gives an overall length of 72.73 m, a wingspan of 79.75 m and a tail height of about 24.1 to 24.3 m. Depending on the weight variant, its maximum takeoff weight is up to 575 tonnes.
