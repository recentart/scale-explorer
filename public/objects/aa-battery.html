    About the AA battery

    The AA is a common cylindrical battery size, designated LR6 by the IEC and 15A by ANSI in its alkaline form. Energizer's datasheet for its E91 alkaline AA gives industry-standard dimensions of 49.50 to 50.50 mm tall and 13.50 to 14.50 mm across. A typical cell weighs 23.0 g and delivers a nominal 1.5 volts.

    • The E91 uses a zinc-manganese dioxide alkaline chemistry with a nominal voltage of 1.5 volts. [1]
    • Energizer rates the E91's shelf life at 10 years at 21 °C. [1]
