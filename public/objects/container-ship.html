    About the container ship

    The biggest container ships are about 400 m long and carry more than 24,000 standard 20-foot containers. Ever Alot, built by China's Hudong-Zhonghua Shipbuilding for Evergreen Marine and delivered in June 2022, measures 399.99 m long and 61.5 m wide. It was the first ship able to carry more than 24,000 TEU, and Guinness World Records lists it among the record holders for container capacity.

    • Ever Alot can carry 24,004 TEU (twenty-foot equivalent units), 12 TEU more than its predecessor Ever Ace. [2]
    • Ever Alot was the seventh ship in Evergreen's series of 13 A-class ships and the first in the world over 24,000 TEU; it is classed by ABS and was deployed on Far East to Europe routes. [1]
    • Guinness World Records lists Ever Alot, Ever Atop and Ever Aria as the largest container ships by capacity, with a gross tonnage of 236,228. [3]
    • According to Guinness, these ships stop just short of 400 m because ships over 400 m count as oversized and pay higher docking fees at many ports. [3]

    Notes on these figures

    • No page from Evergreen or Hudong-Zhonghua itself could be read; the 399.99 m length, 61.5 m width and 33.2 m depth are the builder's figures as reported by MarineLink at delivery.
    • Guinness World Records gives the length as 399.9 m (1,312 ft), slightly different from the 399.99 m reported at delivery.
    • Gross tonnage (236,228) is a measure of enclosed volume, not weight, so it is not stored as a mass. No source gave the ship's actual mass (lightship weight or displacement).
    • The record status quoted is as shown on the Guinness page in September 2026; newer ships may since have been reported with higher capacities.
