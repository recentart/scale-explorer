    About the human egg cell

    The human egg cell, or oocyte, is the female reproductive cell and one of the largest cells in the body. It develops inside a follicle in the ovary and is enclosed by a protective outer coat called the zona pellucida. By ovulation it has reached a final diameter of around 110 to 120 µm, not counting that coat.

    • In a study of immature human oocytes collected from ovarian tissue and matured in the lab, diameters ranged from 60 to 171 µm, with a mean of 115 µm. [1]
    • The same study found that oocytes that reached full maturity (metaphase II) were larger on average than those that did not. [1]
