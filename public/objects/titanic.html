    About the Titanic

    RMS Titanic was a British passenger liner of the White Star Line that sank on its maiden voyage in April 1912. Royal Museums Greenwich gives its overall length as 882.75 ft (269 m) and its breadth as 92.5 ft, with a gross tonnage of 46,329.

    • Titanic's gross tonnage was 46,329 and its net tonnage 21,831. Tonnage measures volume, not weight. [1]
