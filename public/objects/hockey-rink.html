    About the NHL ice hockey rink

    An NHL rink is 200 feet long and 85 feet wide, with corners rounded on a 28-foot radius. The goal lines sit 11 feet in from each end, and the two blue lines are 64 feet out from the goal lines. Each goal opening is 6 feet wide and 4 feet high, and the boards around the ice stand about 42 inches tall.

    • Goal lines are drawn 11 feet from each end of the rink, and the blue lines are 64 feet out from the goal lines, dividing the ice into three zones. [1]
    • Safety glass rises 8 feet above the boards at each end of the rink and at least 6 feet along the sides. [1]
    • An NHL puck is 1 inch thick and 3 inches across and weighs between 5.5 and 6 ounces. [1]
