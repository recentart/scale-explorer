    About the Boeing 737

    The Boeing 737 is a twin-engine, single-aisle airliner family; the 737 MAX series, with CFM LEAP-1B engines and dual-feather Advanced Technology winglets, is its latest generation. Boeing's airport planning document gives the 737-8 an overall length of 129 ft 6 in (39.47 m) and a wingspan of 117 ft 10 in (35.92 m). It seats 178 passengers in a typical two-class layout and can take off at up to 182,200 lb.

    • The 737-8 seats 178 passengers in a two-class layout or 189 in a single class. [1]
    • The 737 MAX family covers the 737-7, 737-8, 737-8-200, 737-9, 737-10 and Business Jet versions, all powered by CFM LEAP-1B engines. [1]
    • The 737 MAX remains an FAA Airplane Design Group III and ICAO Aerodrome Reference Code C aircraft, the same airport category as earlier 737s. [1]
