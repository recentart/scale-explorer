    About the NBA basketball court

    An NBA court is a rectangle 94 feet long and 50 feet wide, measured inside the boundary lines. The basket ring is 18 inches across, with its upper edge set 10 feet above the floor, and hangs from a transparent backboard 6 feet wide and 3.5 feet tall. The three-point arc sits 23 feet 9 inches from the middle of the basket, and the free throw line is 15 feet from the face of the backboard.

    • The NBA playing court is 94 feet long and 50 feet wide, both measured inside the lines, and all lines are 2 inches wide. [1]
    • Each basket ring must have its upper edge 10 feet above the floor, with the nearest inside edge of the ring 6 inches out from the face of the backboard. [1]
    • The backboard is a flat, transparent rectangle 6 feet wide and 3.5 feet tall. [1]
    • The court diagram requires an apron of at least 8 feet beyond the end lines and at least 5 feet beyond the sidelines. [1]
