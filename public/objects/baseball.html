    About the baseball

    A baseball is made of yarn wound around a small cork or rubber core and covered with two strips of white horsehide or cowhide stitched together. Major League Baseball's Official Baseball Rules require it to measure 9 to 9 1/4 inches around. It must weigh between 5 and 5 1/4 ounces.

    • Under Rule 3.01, players may not intentionally discolor or damage the ball, for example by rubbing it with soil, rosin or sandpaper. [1]
