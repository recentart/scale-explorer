    About the 40 ft shipping container

    Standard steel shipping containers are built to external sizes set by the international standard ISO 668, so they stack and lock together on ships, trains and trucks. A 40 ft general-purpose container measures 12,192 mm long, 2,438 mm wide and 2,591 mm (8 ft 6 in) high on the outside. Shipping line Hapag-Lloyd lists its 40 ft boxes at about 3.7 tonnes empty with 67.7 cubic metres of cargo space.

    • ISO 668 sets a minimum internal size for a 40 ft container of 12,029 mm long, 2,350 mm wide and 2,350 mm high. [1]
    • A standard 20 ft container is 6,058 mm long, a little under half the length of a 40 ft container. [1]
    • Some of Hapag-Lloyd's 40 ft general-purpose container series can carry up to 28,800 kg of cargo. [1]
