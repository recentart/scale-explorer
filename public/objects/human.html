    About the average adult

    These are measured averages for U.S. adults aged 20 and older from the CDC's National Center for Health Statistics. The average woman is about 5 ft 3.5 in tall and the average man about 5 ft 9 in. Average body mass runs from about 172 lb for women to about 199 lb for men. Individuals vary widely around these means.

    • The CDC figures are measured values, not self-reported, taken from the report Anthropometric Reference Data for Children and Adults: United States, August 2021 to August 2023. [1]
    • Average waist circumference of U.S. adults aged 20 and older is 40.6 in for men and 38.5 in for women. [1]

    Notes on these figures

    • The figures are U.S. national averages. They are not global averages, and average height and weight differ considerably between countries.
    • The range runs from the female mean to the male mean. It is not the full range of adult sizes.
