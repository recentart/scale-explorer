    About the Boeing 747

    The Boeing 747 'jumbo jet' first flew in 1969 and is known for its hump-shaped upper deck. Its final version, the 747-8, is also the longest: Boeing's airport planning document gives it a length of 250 ft 2 in (76.25 m), a wingspan of 224 ft 5 in (68.40 m) and a maximum takeoff weight of 987,000 lb.
