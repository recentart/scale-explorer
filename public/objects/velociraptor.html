    About the Velociraptor

    Velociraptor was a small, feathered, meat-eating dinosaur that lived in Mongolia and China in the Late Cretaceous, roughly 74 to 70 million years ago. It walked on two legs and had a large sickle-shaped claw on each foot. Museum estimates put it at about 1.8 metres long, far smaller than the animals shown in the Jurassic Park films.

    • The Jurassic Park films showed Velociraptor at about twice its real size, modelled closely on its larger relative Deinonychus. [1]
    • Velociraptor is now thought to have had a fine covering of feather-like structures. [1]
    • Velociraptor mongoliensis was named by Osborn in 1924. [1]
    • Stiffened tendons in its tail helped it keep its balance while striking at prey with one foot raised. [3]

    Notes on these figures

    • All sizes are estimates from fossils.
    • Mass estimates vary a lot: 7 kg (Natural History Museum), 18 kg (AMNH), and 'perhaps no more than 45 kg' (Britannica). The NHM figure is used as the main mass.
    • The AMNH gives the age as about 80 million years ago; the Natural History Museum gives 74-70 million years ago.
