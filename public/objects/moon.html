    About the Moon

    The Moon is Earth's only natural satellite. NASA lists it as 3,475 km across, a little over a quarter of Earth's diameter. Its mass is about 7.35 × 10^22 kg, roughly 1.2 percent of Earth's.

    • The Moon's volumetric mean radius is 1,737.4 km, its equatorial radius 1,738.1 km and its polar radius 1,736.0 km. [1]
    • NASA's fact sheet puts the Moon's mass at 0.0123 times Earth's. [1]
    • NASA describes the Moon as having a radius of about 1,080 miles (1,740 km), less than a third of the width of Earth. [3]

    Notes on these figures

    • The Planetary Fact Sheet's 3,475 km matches twice the Moon's mean radius (1,737.4 km). Twice the equatorial radius on the Moon Fact Sheet (1,738.1 km) would give about 3,476 km. The difference is about 1 km.
