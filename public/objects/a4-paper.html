    About the sheet of A4 paper

    A4 is one of the A-series paper sizes defined in the international standard ISO 216 for stationery and printed matter. The series starts from A0, a sheet with an area of one square metre, and each smaller size is made by cutting the one above it in half parallel to its shorter side. A4 is trimmed to 210 by 297 millimetres.

    • The basic A0 sheet has an area of 1 square metre and measures 841 by 1,189 mm. [1]
    • A3 (297 x 420 mm) is twice the size of A4, and A5 (148 x 210 mm) is half of it. [1]
    • Because each size is half the one above and all sizes share the same shape, two successive A sizes differ in area by a ratio of 2:1. [1]
