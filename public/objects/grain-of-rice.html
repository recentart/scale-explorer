    About the grain of rice

    Rice kernels are sorted into length classes, and long-grain varieties have long, slender kernels. The International Rice Research Institute's Standard Evaluation System classes a hulled brown-rice kernel as long when it measures 6.6 to 7.5 millimetres. Kernels over 7.5 mm count as extra long, while medium grains run 5.51 to 6.6 mm.

    • IRRI's length classes for brown rice are extra long (more than 7.5 mm), long (6.6 to 7.5 mm), medium (5.51 to 6.6 mm) and short (5.5 mm or less). [1]
    • Kernels are called slender when their length is more than 3 times their width. [1]
