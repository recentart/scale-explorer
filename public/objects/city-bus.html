    About the city bus

    New Flyer's Xcelsior low-floor transit bus is built in 35, 40 and 60 ft sizes, and the 40-foot version measures 41 ft 0 in (12.50 m) over the bumpers and 102 in (2.6 m) wide, with a roof height of 10 ft 6 in to 11 ft 1 in depending on the powertrain. It seats up to 40 passengers with room for up to 44 more standing.

    • The 40-foot Xcelsior seats up to 40 passengers and carries up to 44 standees. [2]
    • The bus comes in diesel, hybrid, CNG and battery-electric versions; the curb weight of the 40-foot model ranges from about 27,750 lb (diesel) to 33,805 lb (electric). [1]
    • The wheelbase is 283.75 in (7.2 m), and interior height is 95 in (2.4 m) mid-coach. [1]
    • New Flyer says it has delivered over 6,400 clean diesel buses in North America since 2010. [2]
