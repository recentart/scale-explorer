    About the tennis court

    A regulation tennis court is a rectangle 78 feet (23.77 m) long. It is 27 feet wide for singles and 36 feet wide for doubles, with the extra width forming the doubles alleys along each side. The net hangs 3.5 feet high at the posts and is strapped down to 3 feet at the centre. Service lines sit 21 feet from the net on each side.

    • All tennis court measurements are taken to the outside of the lines. [1]
    • For doubles, the centres of the net posts stand 3 feet outside the doubles court on each side. [1]
    • For international competitions the ITF recommends at least 21 feet of space behind each baseline and at least 12 feet beside each sideline. [1]
