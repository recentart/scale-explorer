    About the semi truck

    A semi truck is a tractor unit pulling a semitrailer. Road designers use the WB-67 'interstate' design vehicle, with a 67 ft wheelbase and an overall length of about 74 ft, to represent long-haul tractor-trailers. Federal rules set the width at 102 inches on the National Network and the gross weight limit at 80,000 lb, while height limits are set by the states.

    • States may not impose an overall length limit on a tractor-semitrailer combination on the National Network. [2]
