    About the typical new house

    The US Census Bureau surveys newly built homes every year. The median new single-family house completed in 2025 had 2,142 square feet of floor area, which is used here as the size of a typical house. New US homes are split almost evenly between one-storey and two-storey designs, with a small share of three storeys or more.

    • About 1,005,000 single-family homes were completed in the US in 2025. [1]
    • The median new single-family home sold in 2025 had 2,194 square feet, slightly more than the median for homes completed. [1]
    • The average floor area of new single-family homes completed in 2025 was 2,378 square feet; regional medians ranged from 1,969 sq ft in the Midwest to 2,459 sq ft in the Northeast. [3]
    • Of new single-family homes completed in 2025, 48% had one storey, 46% had two storeys (a category that includes 1½-storey and split-level houses) and 5% had three or more. [2]
    • The Department of Energy prototype is a two-storey house with 8.5 ft ceilings. [5]

    Notes on these figures

    • The drawing is scaled to the Department of Energy prototype house: 39.8 ft wide with 17 ft walls. The roof above the walls is illustrative, because no authoritative source states a typical roof height or overall house height.
    • No height is given. No authoritative source (building code, planning guidance, government statistics or reputable architecture reference) was found that states a typical overall height for a two-storey house. The Census Bureau publishes the number of storeys, not heights, and building codes set minimum ceiling heights rather than typical house heights.
    • No typical storey height is given. The only government figure found is the 8.5 ft ceiling height that DOE/PNNL assume for their energy-model prototype house. That is a modelling assumption for ceiling height, not a measured floor-to-floor or overall house height, so it appears only as a fact.
    • The floor-area figure applies only to new US homes completed in 2025. Existing US homes and homes in other countries differ, and the median for new homes sold (2,194 sq ft) is slightly higher than for homes completed.
    • The 2,142 sq ft median appears both on the Census highlights page and in the Census detailed square-footage table; both come from the same Census survey.
