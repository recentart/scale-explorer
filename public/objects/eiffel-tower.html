    About the Eiffel Tower

    The Eiffel Tower is Gustave Eiffel's open-lattice wrought-iron tower on the Champ de Mars in Paris, built for the 1889 Exposition Universelle. Broadcast antennas have raised it above its original 312 m, most recently a DAB+ radio antenna added in March 2022, and it now stands 330 m tall. Its four pillars form a square 125 m on each side, and with all its installations it weighs about 10,100 tonnes.

    • Before any antennas were added, the tower was 312 m tall. [1]
    • The metal frame on its own weighs 7,300 tonnes. [1]
    • Each of the four pillars is 25 m wide at ground level. [1]
    • The floors are at 57 m (first), 115 m (second) and 276 m (third) above the ground. [1]
    • The English version of the official site gives the current height as 1,083 feet and the original height without antennas as 1,024 feet. [2]
    • Broadcast antennas raised the tower to 1,052 ft in 1957 and to 1,063 ft in 2000; a 20 ft DAB+ antenna added in March 2022 brought it to 1,083 ft. [3]
    • Construction took 2 years, 2 months and 5 days, from January 28, 1887 to March 31, 1889. [3]
    • The staircase in the East pillar has 1,665 steps from the esplanade to the top. [3]
    • The tower is built almost entirely of open-lattice wrought iron. [4]

    Notes on these figures

    • The live official site toureiffel.paris returns HTTP 403 to automated requests. Its key-figures pages were read from Internet Archive captures made on 10 Sep 2026 (French) and 21 Sep 2026 (English), which are the URLs cited.
    • The French official page gives heights in metres (330 m now, 312 m originally) and the English one gives them in feet (1,083 ft and 1,024 ft). The English page says '10,100 tons' where the French page says '10 100 tonnes', meaning metric tonnes.
    • The press kit is SETE's own document but was read from a third-party copy on tarifs.org. It is undated and mentions works due to finish in 2023, so it probably dates from 2022.
    • Britannica independently gives the same 330 m (1,083 ft) height and 10,100-ton weight. It also describes the tower as 300 m tall standing on a 5 m base, so quoted 'original heights' depend on what is included.
    • The older educational-card PDF hosted by Minecraft Education, cited in the earlier research, was dropped. It is not an official page, and its 'current height' of 324 m is out of date.
