    About the credit card

    Payment cards use the ID-1 size, one of four card sizes defined in the international standard ISO/IEC 7810 for identification cards. An ID-1 card is nominally 85.60 mm wide, 53.98 mm high and 0.76 mm thick. The same standard also defines larger ID-2 and ID-3 sizes for other documents.

    • ISO/IEC 7813, the standard for financial transaction cards, is tested against ID-1 cards. [2]
    • The larger ID-2 and ID-3 sizes are nominally 105.00 x 74.00 mm and 125.00 x 88.00 mm, with the same 0.76 mm thickness. [1]

    • Values are the nominal ID-1 dimensions; the standard allows small tolerances, which are not listed here, and notes its SI and imperial values are rounded separately.
