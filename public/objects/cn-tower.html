    About the CN Tower

    The CN Tower is a concrete communications and observation tower in Toronto, built by Canadian National from February 1973 by more than 1,500 workers over 40 months. It was finished on 2 April 1975 and opened to the public on 26 June 1976, and it remained the world's tallest freestanding structure until the Burj Khalifa overtook it. From its base to the tip of its antenna it measures 553.33 m (1,815.5 ft), with observation levels at 346 m and 447 m.

    • The shaft was cast with a slipform that climbed on hydraulic jacks and gradually narrowed to give the tower its tapered outline. [3]
    • In March 1975 a Sikorsky helicopter nicknamed Olga lifted the antenna into place in 39 pieces. [3]
    • At The Top, 447 m up, the tower can sway by almost half a metre in the wind. [2]
    • The Glass Floor gives a view 342 m straight down, and the high-speed elevators reach the Main Observation Level in 58 seconds at 22 km/h. [2]
