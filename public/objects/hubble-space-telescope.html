    About the Hubble Space Telescope

    The Hubble Space Telescope is a space telescope launched in 1990 that orbits Earth above the atmosphere. NASA gives it a length of 13.2 m (43.5 ft), a width of 4.2 m (14 ft) at the instrument end, and a weight of about 12,246 kg (27,000 lb), roughly the size and weight of a school bus. Its main mirror is 2.4 m across.

    • NASA says Hubble is approximately the same size and weight as a school bus. [1]
    • Hubble's 2.4 m (94.5 in) primary mirror is polished so smoothly that, scaled to the diameter of Earth, no bump would stand more than 15 cm (6 in) tall. [1]
    • Hubble is a Cassegrain reflector powered by two solar arrays. [1]
