    About the soccer ball

    A soccer ball is an air-filled sphere, usually made of stitched or bonded synthetic panels. Law 2 of the Laws of the Game, set by The IFAB, requires a circumference of 68 to 70 cm and a weight of 410 to 450 g at the start of a match. The law also limits its inflation pressure.

    • Law 2 requires a ball pressure of 0.6-1.1 atmosphere (600-1,100 g/cm2) at sea level. [1]
