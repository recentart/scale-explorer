    About the Tyrannosaurus rex

    Tyrannosaurus rex was a large meat-eating dinosaur that lived in what is now the western United States and Canada at the very end of the Cretaceous, about 68 to 66 million years ago. London's Natural History Museum estimates a typical adult at about 12 metres long and 3.5 to 4 metres tall at the hips, weighing roughly 5 to 7 tonnes. The biggest known skeletons, Sue and Scotty, came from animals nearly 13 metres long and over 8 tonnes.

    • Sue, at the Field Museum in Chicago, is the largest and most complete T. rex skeleton found, at around 90 percent complete, and lived to about 28 years old. [2]
    • Barnum Brown found the first known T. rex skeletons in 1900 and 1902, and Henry Fairfield Osborn named the species in 1905. [1]
    • Tooth marks show T. rex fed on Triceratops and Edmontosaurus, and bone fragments turn up in its fossilised dung. [1]
