    About the aircraft carrier

    The Gerald R. Ford class is the newest class of US Navy nuclear-powered aircraft carriers. The Navy lists the lead ship, USS Gerald R. Ford (CVN 78), as 1,106 ft (337 m) long with a 256 ft wide flight deck and a full-load displacement of 100,000 long tons. It was delivered in 2017, and the older Nimitz-class carriers are almost the same size.

    • The Navy fact file says the Nimitz and Gerald R. Ford-class carriers are the largest warships in the world, each designed for about 50 years of service with one mid-life refuelling. [2]

    Notes on these figures

    • The Navy page also lists a "height" of 134 feet, which matches the hull beam given by other sources, so it is not used here.
    • Naval Technology gives 333 m for the class; the Navy figure of 1,106 ft (337 m) for the lead ship is used.
    • The drawing is a simplified side view scaled to the 1,106 ft length; its height is illustrative.
