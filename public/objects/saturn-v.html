    About the Saturn V

    The Saturn V was the rocket NASA used to send Apollo astronauts to the Moon; the Apollo versions had three stages, and a two-stage version launched the Skylab space station in 1973. It was developed at NASA's Marshall Space Flight Center in Huntsville, Alabama. It stood 363 feet (111 m) tall, about as high as a 36-story building, and was 33 feet (396 inches) in diameter. Fully fuelled for liftoff it weighed about 2.8 million kilograms (6.2 million pounds).

    • The Saturn V produced 34.5 million newtons (7.6 million pounds) of thrust at launch. [1]
    • The first Saturn V was launched in 1967 and the last, uncrewed, in 1973 to put the Skylab space station into orbit. [1]
    • NASA gives the Saturn V first stage (S-IC) as 138 feet long and 33 feet in diameter. [3]
    • The Smithsonian's Saturn V, on loan to NASA Johnson Space Center in Houston, is described as the only existing Saturn V with all flyable stages. [2]
