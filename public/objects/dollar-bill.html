    About the US dollar bill

    US paper money has been printed at its present size since the 1928 series, when the older large-format notes were reduced. The Bureau of Engraving and Printing gives that present size as 6.14 by 2.61 inches. A note of any denomination weighs about one gram, and the paper is made of 75% cotton and 25% linen.

    • Before the 1928 series, US notes were larger, measuring 7.375 by 3.125 inches. [1]
    • A note can take about 4,000 double folds (forward, then backward) before it tears. [2]
    • Currency paper is 75% cotton and 25% linen. [2]
