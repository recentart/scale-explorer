    About the E. coli bacterium

    Escherichia coli is a rod-shaped bacterium that lives in the gut and is one of the most studied organisms in biology. Its shape is close to a cylinder with rounded ends. A widely used rule of thumb puts a cell at about 2 µm long and 1 µm wide, with a volume near 1 cubic micrometre and a mass of about a picogram, though cell size changes with growth conditions.

    • Faster-growing cells are larger: E. coli dry mass differs about five-fold between slow- and fast-growing cultures. [1]
    • Treating the cell as a cylinder with hemispherical caps, 1 µm wide and 2 µm long, gives a volume of about 1.3 µm³. [1]
