    • Polar bear Length (head and body): 1.8–2.7 m
    • Ostrich Height (adult male): 2.1–2.7 m
    • Cow (Holstein) Height at shoulder: ≈ 1.5 m

    • African lion Shoulder height: 1.1–1.2 m
    • Great white shark Length (average adult): 3.4–4.9 m
    • Hippopotamus Length: 3.3–5 m
    • African elephant Shoulder height: 2.5–3.7 m
